# Marswalk Planner: Demo Video Script (about 2 min)

Record your screen with the live site open (https://0xr4j.github.io/mars-walk-planner/) in a full-width desktop browser. Speak the **VO** lines. **ON SCREEN** says what to do.

---

### 0:00–0:15 · Hook
**ON SCREEN:** Page loads, zoomed out on Jezero Crater.
**VO:** "The first astronauts on Mars will step out of their habitat onto ground no human has ever walked. NASA has decades of Mars data, but it's spread across missions and built for global views, not for one astronaut's walk. So we built **Marswalk Planner**."

### 0:15–0:40 · Layered NASA data
**ON SCREEN:** In the left panel, toggle the layers one by one: HiRISE on and off, THEMIS Night IR on with its opacity slider moved, then the MOLA hillshade. Zoom into the crater floor.
**VO:** "We picked Jezero Crater, where Perseverance is exploring an ancient lake delta. Every layer streams live from NASA's Mars Trek. That's optical imagery from Viking, CTX and HiRISE down to 25 cm a pixel, thermal data from Mars Odyssey's THEMIS and the TES instrument, and topography from MOLA and HRSC. THEMIS at night shows thermal inertia, so bright areas are solid rock and dark areas are loose sand. That tells the crew where it's safe to walk."

### 0:40–1:05 · The planned EVA
**ON SCREEN:** Click the habitat icon, then science stops 1 through 4 in the right panel. Open the delta-front popup.
**VO:** "The habitat sits near Perseverance's landing site. The planned walk visits four science stops: Séítah's olivine rocks, the Máaz lava floor, the delta front, which is our best bet for biosignatures, and a field of flood-carried boulders. Each stop shows why we're going there, which data supports it, and what kit to bring."

### 1:05–1:30 · Safety engine
**ON SCREEN:** Point at the red and yellow hazard zones and the green walk-back ring. Change the walk speed or EVA hours and watch the GO status update. Click **Add waypoints** and draw a route through the red dune zone so the status flips to **NO-GO**.
**VO:** "Safety comes first. Red zones are no-go dunes where a suit or rover could sink. Yellow zones are scarps and boulder fields. The planner works out walking time, science time and consumables margin using Mars's real radius, not Earth's. If I draw a route through the dunes, it goes NO-GO right away. The green ring shows how far the crew can walk and still get home with a one-hour reserve."

### 1:30–1:45 · Space weather
**ON SCREEN:** Scroll to the Space Weather panel.
**VO:** "Mars has no magnetic field to shield against radiation. We pull live solar flare, CME and particle-event data from NASA's DONKI API. If a solar particle event hits, the crew gets told to shelter."

### 1:45–2:00 · HUD mode and close
**ON SCREEN:** Click **HUD mode**.
**VO:** "Out on the surface, the same map becomes a helmet heads-up display showing the next stop, consumables, route and radiation status. Marswalk Planner turns NASA's Mars archive into a map one astronaut can follow, safely. Thank you."

---

**Recording tips:** Keep it under 2 minutes. Zoom the browser to 110% so text is readable. Do one practice run, because tiles load faster the second time (they're cached).
